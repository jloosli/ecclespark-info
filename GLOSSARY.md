# Ecclespark Info

A public page where visitors find the link to the weekly Meeting's Broadcast, plus occasional Announcements, kept up to date by a few Editors.

## Language

**Meeting**:
The recurring weekly gathering the site exists to point people to.
_Avoid_: Service, event

**Broadcast**:
One dated occurrence of the Meeting, with the link (Zoom or YouTube) visitors use to watch or join it.
_Avoid_: Stream, session, event

**Announcement**:
An occasional notice shown to visitors alongside the Broadcasts.
_Avoid_: Notice, post, news

**Editor**:
A person who signs in to add Broadcasts and Announcements.
_Avoid_: Admin, user, author
